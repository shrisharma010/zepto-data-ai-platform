Refunds for eligible items are processed immediately back to the original payment method upon approval. Depending on your bank, credit/debit card refunds may take 3 to 5 business days to reflect. UPI refunds usually settle within 2 to 4 hours. Perishable items such as fruits, vegetables, dairy, and meat are not eligible for physical return, but refund requests for spoiled or missing items are accepted within 2 hours of delivery.
